What It Is

Co-managed IT extends your internal team — it doesn't take it over

Most growing Los Angeles businesses reach a point where their IT person or small team is good at the job but simply spread too thin. Tickets pile up during projects. Coverage stops at 6 PM, which means evenings, weekends, and vacations are exposed. Specialized work — serious cybersecurity, a cloud migration, a compliance deadline — needs depth that one or two generalists can't reasonably carry. Hiring another full-time engineer is expensive and slow, and it still doesn't solve the around-the-clock problem.

Co-managed IT solves this differently. Pro Link Systems works alongside your internal staff, taking the layers that are hard to cover in-house while your team keeps ownership of the systems, applications, and relationships closest to your business. Your people hold the context no outside provider can buy — which systems the business can't lose, how approvals really work, who to call about what. We supply the capacity, the specialized skills, and the 24/7/365 coverage. Where that split lands is up to you, and it can change as your needs do.

It's a practical middle ground between going it alone with a stretched internal team and handing everything to a provider. If you have no internal IT at all, our managed IT services and outsourced IT options are usually the better fit. If you have capable internal IT that needs backup, depth, and coverage, co-managed IT is built for exactly that.

How Our Co-Managed Model Works

A partnership scoped around your gaps

1

Common Questions

Co-managed IT — answered

Co-managed IT is a partnership in which an outside IT provider works alongside your existing internal IT person or team, rather than replacing them. You keep the people who know your business, and you add the tools, specialized skills, and around-the-clock coverage that are hard for a small internal team to maintain on its own. The split is flexible: Pro Link can take help desk overflow and after-hours tickets, run 24/7 monitoring and security, lead a cloud migration or other project, or provide vCIO-level strategy — while your internal staff keep ownership of the systems and relationships closest to the business.
Fully managed or outsourced IT means an external provider handles essentially all of your technology, which fits businesses with no internal IT staff. Co-managed IT is for companies that already have an internal IT person or team and want to extend it — adding coverage, specialized expertise, and capacity without hiring, and without giving up the in-house knowledge they already have. If you have no internal IT, our managed IT and outsourced IT options are usually the better starting point; if you have internal IT that is stretched, co-managed is designed for exactly that situation.

Co-managed engagements are built to fit the gaps in your internal team. Common components include help desk overflow and after-hours or holiday coverage from our in-house, US-based 24/7 help desk; proactive monitoring and patching; cybersecurity with SentinelOne-based EDR and 24/7 SOC analysts; cloud and Microsoft 365 management; tested backup and disaster recovery; vCIO strategy, budgeting, and vendor management; and project support such as migrations, rollouts, and office moves.

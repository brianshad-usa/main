Common questions

Are we too small to work with Pro Link Systems?

No. Our services are designed for organizations with about 10 to 100 users, and several of our longest relationships are with organizations of 30 to 60 people. If your team is smaller or larger, talk to us anyway; we will tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.

Do we need an internal IT department?

No. We can serve as your complete IT department, as we have for The Waverly School since 2003. If you already have IT staff, we can support them instead through co-managed IT.

Can you work alongside our in-house IT staff?

Yes. Our co-managed IT option adds our in-house, US-based help desk, monitoring, security and project capacity to your existing team, while your IT lead keeps control of priorities.

How predictable are monthly IT costs?

Managed IT services are priced per user at a flat monthly rate, with unlimited support and no per-ticket charges. Cybersecurity is built into every managed IT plan, and any one-time onboarding costs are disclosed in writing before you sign. Our managed IT pricing guide explains typical ranges in Los Angeles.

Can your services grow with our business?

Yes. Per-user pricing scales with your team, and services can be added as your needs change. The Law Offices of Alan B. Snitzer, for example, has relied on us since 2006 as the firm grew to almost 30 people.

How difficult is it to switch from our current IT provider?

We handle transitions regularly. Onboarding begins with a full audit of your current environment, and we coordinate directly with your outgoing provider when needed. When a dispute with its previous provider left Cooke’s Crating without administrative access to its own systems, we moved its entire environment in five days with zero downtime.

Will we get the same attention as your larger clients?

Yes. Every client reaches the same in-house, US-based team, with calls answered live during business hours. Your systems are documented, so whoever helps you has the full context, and your technology plans are reviewed with you regularly.

How fast do you respond to IT issues?

Pro Link Systems’ average ticket first-response time is 15 minutes. Calls are answered live by our in-house, US-based team during business hours, not an offshore call center, and managed-service clients reach the on-call help desk after hours by pressing 1.

What areas of Los Angeles do you serve?

We serve organizations throughout Greater Los Angeles, including Woodland Hills, Burbank, Glendale, Santa Monica, Pasadena, Long Beach, Torrance and Culver City, as well as Ventura and Orange counties.

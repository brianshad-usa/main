Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask our team

How much does managed IT services cost in Los Angeles?

It depends on the number of users, the scope of services and the complexity of your environment, so every engagement is scoped after a consultation. Our managed IT pricing guide explains the common pricing models and typical ranges in Los Angeles.

How does an engagement with Pro Link Systems work?

Engagements run under a Managed Services Agreement that defines scope and responsibilities. We start by documenting your environment, agree on priorities with your leadership team, and review progress and plans with you regularly.

Can you work alongside our in-house IT staff?

Yes. Our co-managed IT option adds our 24/7 in-house, US-based help desk, monitoring, security and project capacity to your existing team, while your IT lead keeps control of priorities.

How fast do you respond to IT issues?

Pro Link Systems’ average ticket first-response time is 15 minutes, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Calls are answered live by our in-house, US-based team, not a phone tree or an offshore call center.

What areas of Los Angeles do you serve?

We serve organizations throughout Greater Los Angeles, including Woodland Hills, Burbank, Glendale, Santa Monica, Pasadena, Long Beach, Torrance and Culver City, as well as Ventura and Orange counties.

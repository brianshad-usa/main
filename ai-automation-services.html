Our approach

Start with the workflow, not the tool.

Many AI projects stall because they begin with the technology instead of the problem. Ours begin with the work, and include the change management that gets a system used.

  1. Audit and prioritize

    We map current workflows, identify manual bottlenecks and rank each opportunity by value, complexity and time to benefit. You receive a prioritized roadmap before anything is built.

  2. Security and privacy

    Data residency, access controls, audit logging and privacy boundaries are designed at the start of the deployment.

  3. Build and test

    We build inside your existing environment, including Azure, Microsoft 365, your CRM and your ERP, and test with real data before rollout.

  4. Train and support

    Staff training, documentation and a feedback loop that improves the system over time, with our team engaged after launch.

Common questions

AI and automation, answered.

Does our data leave our environment when using AI tools?

Private assistants are designed so that it does not. We deploy private RAG and AI assistants within your own Microsoft Azure tenant or on-premises environment, using isolated deployments of large language models or Microsoft's enterprise AI services, so your documents, policies and business data stay under your control rather than going to public AI services.

What is a RAG system and why does my business need one?

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It is an AI approach that lets your team ask questions in plain English and get answers drawn from your own documents, policies, manuals and data rather than from the public internet. Instead of searching SharePoint folders or emailing HR for a policy, staff ask a private assistant and see the answer with its source. We build these inside your environment.

What kinds of workflows can you automate?

Any repetitive, rule-based task that consumes staff time is a candidate for automation. Common examples include document intake and routing, invoice processing, onboarding checklists, IT ticket triage, report generation, data extraction from PDFs and emails, approval workflows, and CRM data entry. We audit your workflows first to identify the highest-value opportunities before building anything.

How does Microsoft Copilot deployment work?

Microsoft 365 Copilot requires an eligible Microsoft 365 business or enterprise plan plus a Copilot license, proper security configuration, and permissions cleanup before it goes live. We handle the licensing review, identity and access review, SharePoint permissions audit, security hardening, pilot rollout and user training. Without this preparation, Copilot can surface sensitive files to people who should not see them.

How do you keep AI tools from exposing sensitive data?

We start with permissions, because an AI assistant can surface anything a user already has access to. Before anything goes live, we clean up SharePoint and file-share permissions, enforce MFA and role-based access, turn on audit logging, and set up sensitive data detection. Private deployments run inside your own Azure tenant or on-premises environment. If your business is regulated, we work alongside your compliance advisor and handle the IT side.

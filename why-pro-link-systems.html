Common questions

Questions when comparing IT providers.

How is Pro Link Systems different from a national IT company?

We work in one region. Pro Link Systems has been headquartered in Woodland Hills since 1999 and serves organizations across Greater Los Angeles, Ventura County and Orange County. Our help desk is in-house and US-based and available 24/7 to managed-service clients under contract, and same-day on-site support is available when needed. A national provider brings scale; we bring accountability, continuity and local presence.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Pro Link Systems does not require long-term contracts. Services are governed by our Managed Services Agreement, and clients stay because the service works.

How fast do you respond to IT emergencies?

Managed-service clients can reach our in-house help desk at any hour: calls are answered live during business hours, and after hours clients press 1 to reach the on-call help desk. Our average ticket first-response time is 15 minutes, 90% of issues are resolved on first contact, and average resolution time is under an hour. Same-day on-site support is available when needed.

What happens if my dedicated engineer leaves Pro Link Systems?

Your documentation stays with Pro Link Systems, not with any individual. We maintain records of your systems, configurations and history so that another member of our team can support you with full context.

Can you take over from our current IT provider?

Yes. We handle managed IT transitions regularly. Onboarding begins with a full audit of your current environment, including hardware, software, network and security, so we understand what we are inheriting. We coordinate directly with your outgoing provider when needed.

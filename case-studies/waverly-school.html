Company profile

The Waverly School is an independent, accredited educational institution in Pasadena, California, serving students from preschool through 12th grade. Operating across three campuses, the school provides a progressive, college-preparatory educational experience emphasizing intellectual development, creativity, critical thinking and social responsibility.

The challenge

A school’s technology has to serve two very different populations at once: staff who depend on reliable administrative systems, and students who hold their own accounts. The environment has to be secure, easy to administer and dependable across the school year, without a large in-house IT team to run it.

Our approach

  • Pro Link Systems acts as The Waverly School’s outsourced IT department
  • Management and support of the school’s Google Workspace environment, covering both staff and student accounts

Outcomes

  • A relationship since 2003, among Pro Link Systems’ longest-tenured

Long-term value

A dependable technology environment for staff and students, run by the same outside partner for more than two decades.

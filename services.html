Capabilities

What we deliver.

01 · IT operations

24/7 help desk and IT operations

When something breaks, your people reach an experienced technician, not a ticket queue. Our help desk is run by our own in-house, US-based team that knows your environment, and is available 24/7 to managed-service clients under contract.

Our average ticket first-response time is 15 minutes, first-contact resolution is 90%, and average resolution time is under an hour. Complex issues go straight to senior engineers.

Help desk details
  • Remote support, with same-day on-site support when needed
  • Proactive monitoring that catches many issues before users notice
  • A dedicated account team that knows your business
  • Ticket status visible through our client portal
  • Published help desk metrics, so you know what to expect

02 · Cybersecurity & risk

Cybersecurity and threat protection

Layered defenses across identity, endpoints, email, network and cloud stop threats before they become incidents, backed by monitoring, documented controls and a documented incident response plan.

If your organization is regulated, we work alongside your compliance advisor and handle the IT side: access controls, encryption, patching, monitoring and backups verified by restore testing.

Cybersecurity details
  • Security assessments with a prioritized remediation plan
  • MFA, conditional access and identity management
  • Endpoint detection and response, email protection and patching
  • Managed firewalls, network segmentation and secure remote access
  • Security awareness training and phishing simulation
  • 24/7 threat monitoring and alerting

03 · Cloud & infrastructure

Cloud architecture and infrastructure

We design, migrate and manage environments on Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, hybrid configurations and virtual desktops. Every architecture decision is driven by your workloads, security requirements and budget, not by what is easiest to manage.

Cloud details
  • Microsoft 365 deployment and management
  • Azure migrations, lift-and-shift or re-architected
  • Hybrid environments that keep on-premises systems where they make sense
  • Network design: SD-WAN, VLANs and structured cabling
  • Phased migrations with rollback plans
  • Identity governance, conditional access and encryption by default

04 · Business continuity

Business continuity and disaster recovery

Hardware fails, ransomware happens and people delete things. We design backup architectures where recovery is the default, not the last resort, and verify backups by restore testing rather than trusting job reports.

Continuity details
  • Automated backups: on-site, off-site and cloud-replicated
  • Defined recovery time and recovery point objectives
  • Immutable backups that ransomware cannot encrypt
  • Microsoft 365 backup for Teams, SharePoint and Exchange
  • Recovery drills that confirm your targets are realistic

05 · Strategy

Strategic consulting and virtual CIO

Technology decisions made without a strategy waste money and create technical debt. As your virtual CIO we join leadership meetings, advise on major investments, manage vendors and keep your roadmap aligned with where the business is going.

Virtual CIO details
  • Technology roadmaps over 12 to 36 months, with realistic budgets
  • Vendor management and accountability
  • Objective audits of your technology and spend
  • Risk and investment reporting for leadership
  • Hardware as a Service for predictable device refresh

06 · Modern workplace

Secure remote and hybrid work

Whether teams are remote, hybrid or traveling, we build workplaces that stay productive and secure wherever people work, without trading security for convenience.

Identity & access details
  • Secure remote access: VPN, ZTNA and conditional access
  • Virtual desktops delivered to any device
  • Microsoft Teams deployment and governance
  • Device management, enrollment and remote wipe
  • Remote provisioning for new hires, immediate revocation for departures

07 · AI & automation

AI and process automation

We help organizations evaluate, deploy and govern AI inside their existing environment, focused on practical use cases: automating workflows, improving responsiveness and getting value from existing data, without compromising privacy or security.

AI & automation details
  • Microsoft Copilot readiness, rollout and training
  • Workflow automation for manual, repetitive processes
  • Private AI knowledge assistants
  • AI governance and acceptable-use policies
  • Custom automation with Power Automate and scripted workflows

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much does managed IT services cost in Los Angeles?

It depends on the number of users, the scope of services and the complexity of your environment, so every engagement is scoped after a consultation. Our managed IT pricing guide explains the common pricing models and typical ranges in Los Angeles.

How does an engagement with Pro Link Systems work?

Engagements run under a Managed Services Agreement that defines scope and responsibilities. We start by documenting your environment, agree on priorities with your leadership team, and review progress and plans with you regularly.

Can you work alongside our in-house IT staff?

Yes. Our co-managed IT option adds our 24/7 in-house, US-based help desk, monitoring, security and project capacity to your existing team, while your IT lead keeps control of priorities.

How fast do you respond to IT issues?

Pro Link Systems’ average ticket first-response time is 15 minutes. Calls are answered live by our in-house, US-based team during business hours, not an offshore call center, and managed-service clients reach the on-call help desk after hours by pressing 1.

What areas of Los Angeles do you serve?

We serve organizations throughout Greater Los Angeles, including Woodland Hills, Burbank, Glendale, Santa Monica, Pasadena, Long Beach, Torrance and Culver City, as well as Ventura and Orange counties.

A technology consultation is the simplest place to start. We’ll review your environment and priorities and recommend the right engagement, whether that is fully managed, co-managed or a single initiative.

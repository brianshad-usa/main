§ 3Information We Collect

Information you provide to us. When you fill out a contact or consultation form, book a meeting, or reach out by phone or email, we collect the information you choose to give us: typically your name, work email address, phone number, company name, and your message or request. Some forms also ask about your organization, such as its size or industry.

How you reached us. When you submit a form, it also sends us the page you submitted it from, the page you first arrived on and the site that referred you, any campaign tags in the link you followed (for example utm_source), and any ad click identifier added by Google (GCLID, GBRAID or WBRAID) or Microsoft (MSCLKID). Our server adds your approximate city and region, derived from your IP address by Cloudflare, and whether you used a mobile or desktop device. This tells us which pages and ads lead to inquiries.

Information collected automatically. When you visit, the Site’s infrastructure records standard technical data: your IP address, browser type and device information, the pages you view, and an approximate (city-level) location derived from your IP address. The analytics and advertising services described in Section 4 also collect information about your visit, subject to your choices.

Microsoft 365 Security Assessment orders. If you order the assessment, we collect the contact details of the person ordering and of your technical contact, your company name and domain, approximate employee count, any notes you add, your billing name and address, the name and title of the person authorizing the engagement, and the IP address the order came from. Your card is entered in Authorize.net’s own secure payment form; we do not receive or store card numbers.

Meeting bookings. If you book a meeting through our Microsoft Bookings page, Microsoft collects the details you enter (such as your name, email and phone) on our behalf.

What we do not collect. The Site has no user accounts or logins and does not ask for sensitive personal information. Please do not submit confidential details through our web forms.

§ 4Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies (such as pixels and local storage) in four groups. You control the analytics and advertising groups with Your Privacy Choices.

Strictly necessary. Cloudflare delivers and protects the Site. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that form submissions come from a person rather than a bot, using technical signals from your browser; it is not used for advertising. During checkout, Cloudflare and Authorize.net process what is needed to take your order and payment. These are always on because the Site and its forms cannot work securely without them. Cloudflare Web Analytics also counts page views without cookies and without identifying individual visitors.

Analytics. Google Analytics 4 tells us how visitors use the Site: pages viewed, time on page, approximate location, device type and how you arrived. On some pages, Microsoft Clarity and Hotjar record how pages are used (scrolling, clicks and mouse movement) so we can improve them; they are set up not to record what you type into form fields, and they load only when analytics is allowed.

Advertising measurement. Our Google Ads tag and Microsoft Advertising (UET) tag tell us when a visit or an action on the Site, such as submitting a form, clicking our phone number or completing an order, followed a click on one of our ads. These tags can set cookies and receive your IP address, browser data, the pages you visit on the Site and ad click identifiers. Google and Microsoft may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites when you visit our Site.

Hashed contact details (enhanced conversions). When you submit a form and advertising measurement is allowed, our Google tag takes the name, email address and phone number you entered, normalizes them (for example, lowercasing the email address and formatting the phone number), and converts them with a one-way hash (SHA-256) in your browser before sending them to Google over an encrypted connection. Google uses the hashed values to match your inquiry to an ad click from a signed-in Google user, so we can measure which ads work. Hashing is a security measure, not anonymization: hashed contact details are still personal information, because Google can match them against data it already holds. Google uses this data to provide measurement to us under its enhanced conversions data use terms, and says it does not share it with other advertisers. If you opt out, or do not opt in where consent is required, the Site does not send these details.

Offline conversion measurement. When an inquiry that came from an ad becomes a qualified opportunity or a client, we may report that to Google using only the ad click identifier, the date and an estimated value, through Google’s Data Manager. We do not send your name or contact details in these reports.

No remarketing or customer lists. We do not use remarketing or personalized-ad audiences, and the Site tells Google not to use your visit for ad personalization. We do not upload customer contact lists to advertising platforms.

Embedded content. Some pages show a YouTube video (in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode) and our contact page shows a Google map. These load content from Google, which receives your IP address and browser data and may set cookies when you play the video or use the map, under Google’s own policy.

Provider policies. How Google uses information from sites that use its services · Microsoft Privacy Statement · Hotjar privacy policy · Cloudflare privacy policy · Authorize.net privacy policy

§ 5How We Use Information
  • To respond to your inquiries, schedule consultations you request, and send you a confirmation of your request.
  • To provide, quote, deliver and administer our services, including the Microsoft 365 Security Assessment.
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve it (analytics).
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising, including matching inquiries to ad clicks (Section 4).
  • To operate, secure and protect the Site and our forms from fraud and abuse.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not use information collected through the Site for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

§ 6How We Share Information

We disclose information only for the purposes in Section 5:

  • Service providers that operate our systems. Microsoft (our business email and Microsoft Bookings, where your messages and bookings are received and stored); Cloudflare (website hosting, security, spam protection and checkout processing); Authorize.net (card payments); and GitHub (a private repository where our team manages assessment orders, including the ordering contact’s name, email, phone and IP address).
  • Analytics providers. Google (Google Analytics), Microsoft (Clarity) and Hotjar, as described in Section 4.
  • Advertising partners. Google (Google Ads, including hashed contact details for enhanced conversions and ad click identifiers for offline conversion measurement) and Microsoft (Microsoft Advertising), to measure our advertising, as described in Section 4.
  • Legal requirements. When required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights, safety or property.
  • Business transfers. If our business is merged, acquired or reorganized, information may transfer as part of that transaction.

"Selling" and "sharing." We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not disclose it to third parties for their own direct marketing. California law, however, defines "selling" broadly and defines "sharing" as disclosing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Making identifiers (such as cookie identifiers, IP addresses and hashed contact details) and website activity available to Google and Microsoft through their advertising tags may fall within those definitions. In the past 12 months, the categories involved were identifiers, internet or other electronic network activity, and approximate location. You can opt out at any time; see Section 7.

§ 7Your Privacy Choices

Your Privacy Choices. Use the Your Privacy Choices link (also at the bottom of every page) to allow or turn off analytics and advertising measurement. Turning off advertising measurement opts you out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for advertising: the Site then tells Google and Microsoft not to use advertising cookies, stops sending hashed contact details from forms, and asks Google to limit how it processes data from your visit. Your choice is saved in your browser, so it applies on this device and browser until you change it or clear your browser’s storage.

Default settings. If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics and advertising measurement stay off unless you agree to them. Elsewhere they are on unless you opt out.

Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, and advertising measurement stays off for that browser.

Do Not Track. There is no common standard for browser "Do Not Track" signals, so the Site does not respond to them. We do honor Global Privacy Control as described above.

Other controls. You can block or delete cookies in your browser’s settings; the Site remains fully usable without analytics and advertising cookies. You can also manage how Google uses your data at myadcenter.google.com, and Microsoft at account.microsoft.com/privacy.

Marketing email. Every marketing or outreach email we send includes a working unsubscribe option; you can also reply and ask us to stop.

§ 8Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know and access the personal information we hold about you, including the categories, sources, purposes and recipients;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Delete personal information, subject to legal exceptions (for example, records we must keep for tax purposes);
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising (Section 7); and
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How to make a request. Email info@prolinksystems.com or call 1-800-890-6133. To protect your information, we will confirm your identity before acting, usually by confirming details you gave us, such as the email address you used to contact us. You may use an authorized agent; we will ask for your signed permission and may confirm your identity directly with you. We confirm requests to know, correct or delete within 10 business days and respond within 45 days (or tell you if we need up to 45 more days). Opt-out requests take effect immediately through Your Privacy Choices, and within 15 business days when made by email or phone.

California residents may exercise these rights under California law, and we will not discriminate against anyone for doing so.

§ 9Data Retention
  • Inquiries and correspondence (form submissions, emails and bookings) are kept as long as they are useful for the request or relationship they relate to, and as required for legal, accounting or record-keeping obligations.
  • Marketing measurement records (how an inquiry reached us and its outcome) are kept as long as needed to measure our marketing results. Google accepts offline conversion reports only for ad clicks from the previous 90 days.
  • Checkout records held by our order system are deleted automatically 7 days after an unpaid checkout and 45 days after payment.
  • Assessment order records are kept for the engagement and as long as needed to support it, and then as required for tax and accounting.
  • Google Analytics keeps event-level data for no longer than 14 months. Other analytics and advertising providers keep data under their own retention schedules (see their policies in Section 4).
§ 10Security

Security is our profession. The Site is served only over HTTPS, and we apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to the information we hold, including access controls, encrypted transport and hardened business systems. Our server logs do not record the content of your messages, and order records are posted to our private order workflow only after we confirm that it is not publicly accessible. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we protect your information with the same discipline we bring to our clients’ environments.

§ 11Children’s Privacy

The Site is a business-to-business website and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not sell or share the personal information of anyone we know to be under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

§ 12Visitors Outside the United States

Our services are offered to organizations in the United States, and the information described here is processed in the United States. If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics and advertising measurement stay off unless you agree to them (Section 7).

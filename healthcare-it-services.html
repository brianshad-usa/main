The Healthcare IT Problem

Healthcare IT Failures
Put Patients at Risk — Not Just Data.

When the EHR is down, a workstation is locked by ransomware or nobody answers the IT line, patients wait and the schedule falls behind. A medical or dental office can't put IT problems on hold until tomorrow.

Ransomware Targeting Patient Records

A single successful attack can encrypt your EHR and imaging data, push your team back to paper charts and put patient information at risk.

Unsecured Connected Medical Devices

IoT infusion pumps, imaging systems, and patient monitors are often unpatched and flat-networked with clinical workstations. A compromised device can serve as an entry point into your entire network.

IT Support That Doesn't Pick Up

Phone trees, hold queues and next-day callbacks leave your front desk and clinical staff stuck while patients wait. When a login, printer or EHR connection fails mid-appointment, every minute counts.

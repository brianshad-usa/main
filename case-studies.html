Global Holdings

IndustryHealthcare — diagnostic medical imaging (MRI and CT scanning focus); one of the largest medical imaging providers in Southern California
LocationSouthern California — 19 owned facilities, plus a referral network of hundreds of joint-venture / subsidiary facilities
Company size200+ employees across 19 locations
RelationshipPro Link Systems engagement of 12+ years, ending in 2021 when Global Holdings was dissolved and its facilities were sold

The Challenge

Global Holdings was one of Southern California's largest diagnostic imaging providers, handling medical imaging of all kinds with a particular focus on MRI and CT. Running 19 owned facilities — and coordinating with hundreds of partner facilities to which its team referred patients — created a demanding technical problem: imaging studies (DICOM) generated on many different modalities, in many locations and across many ownership arrangements, all had to move reliably into one place for processing and clinician access.

On top of the logistics, this was protected health information. As a healthcare organization, Global Holdings needed airtight security and privacy around patient records and imaging data — in an industry that has seen repeated, high-profile breaches — while giving referring doctors and patients convenient, appropriate access to studies.

The Solution

Pro Link Systems served as Global Holdings' technology partner for over a decade, designing and operating the full imaging and IT backbone:

  • Network designed from the ground up across all 19 owned facilities to carry imaging workloads reliably
  • DICOM ingestion from every modality, every location — uploading imaging studies from all facilities into a central environment
  • A cloud environment architected from scratch on AWS, designed and managed by Pro Link, where the imagery was consolidated for processing and access
  • Joint-venture / referral image transfer — handling image transfer into the cloud from the hundreds of partner facilities (owned by other medical groups) where Global Holdings referred patients, so studies from those sites reached the same processing and access environment
  • A secure patient portal enabling doctors and patients to access imaging studies online in a secure, compliant manner
  • A security-and-privacy-first posture around patient records and data throughout — the central focus of the engagement given the healthcare context

The Results

  • A 12+ year partnership supporting 200+ employees across 19 facilities and a referral network of hundreds of partner sites
  • A clean security record: no cybersecurity incident at any point across the entire 12+ year engagement — in an industry where imaging and healthcare providers have frequently been breached
  • A single consolidated AWS environment receiving DICOM studies from every owned modality and from hundreds of joint-venture facilities
  • Secure online access to studies for referring physicians and patients via the Pro Link–designed patient portal
  • Thousands of studies were conducted per month; they had average of 5 modality per site.

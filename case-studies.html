Review R1

Precision manufacturing · Current client

Thirteen years of technology governance, through growth and an acquisition.

Background

Cunico is a precision manufacturer with a legacy of more than 70 years, supplying mission-critical valves, manifolds and fittings. In April 2022 it was acquired by BWX Technologies (BWXT) and became part of its Government Operations segment.

The challenge

As Cunico grew, it needed rock-solid technology governance and infrastructure that matched the standards of the platforms it supplies. After the acquisition, its systems also had to align with enterprise-grade practices while staying reliable day to day.

Our approach

  • Architected, implemented and maintained local and cloud-native environments across Cunico’s multiple sites
  • Hardened its cybersecurity posture, with continuous monitoring and defense of critical systems
  • Designed and enforced workflows, backup and disaster-recovery strategies and access controls as the company scaled
  • Supported the integration into BWXT, aligning systems with enterprise-grade practices

Outcomes and long-term value

  • A continuously renewed partnership of more than 13 years
  • A well-governed environment that carried Cunico through its integration into BWXT

Review R4

Healthcare imaging · Past engagement

A secure imaging backbone for 19 facilities and hundreds of partner sites.

The challenge

Global Holdings ran 19 owned diagnostic imaging facilities and referred patients to hundreds of partner facilities owned by other medical groups. Imaging studies produced on many modalities, in many locations and under many ownership arrangements all had to reach one place for processing and clinician access.

Complexity

This was protected health information, in an industry that has seen repeated, high-profile breaches. Patient records and imaging data needed airtight security and privacy, while referring doctors and patients needed convenient, appropriate access to studies.

Our approach

  • A network designed from the ground up across all 19 owned facilities
  • DICOM ingestion from every modality at every location
  • A cloud environment on AWS, architected and managed by Pro Link, consolidating imagery for processing and access
  • Image transfer from hundreds of joint-venture and referral facilities into the same environment
  • A secure, access-controlled patient portal for referring doctors and patients
  • A security-and-privacy-first posture throughout the engagement

Outcomes

  • A partnership of more than 12 years supporting 200+ employees across 19 facilities
  • One consolidated environment receiving studies from every owned modality and hundreds of partner sites
  • Thousands of imaging studies handled each month
  • No cybersecurity incident at any point during the engagement

Legal · Current client

From an on-premises data center to Azure, across 18 years of growth.

The challenge

The firm’s environment started entirely on premises, in a data center at its own office. As the practice grew, so did the demands on that infrastructure: more staff, more data, higher security expectations and a series of office relocations, each of which meant moving critical systems without disrupting the firm’s work or its confidentiality obligations.

Our approach

  • Many migrations and upgrades across the entire environment as the practice scaled
  • A move from the on-premises data center to the cloud: today, over 90% of the firm’s environment runs on Microsoft Azure
  • A premium security umbrella around the firm’s data, appropriate to a law practice’s confidentiality obligations
  • The infrastructure move handled for every office relocation

Outcomes and long-term value

  • Over 90% of the environment running on Azure
  • Multiple office relocations completed with Pro Link managing the infrastructure
  • An 18-year relationship that continues today

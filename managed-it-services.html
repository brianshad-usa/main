Los Angeles managed IT pricing

What do managed IT services in Los Angeles cost?

Pro Link Systems uses flat-rate, per-user pricing: unlimited support, monitoring around the clock and cybersecurity for one predictable monthly fee. Most Los Angeles businesses with 5 to 150 employees land between $75 and $200 per user per month, depending on their security needs and the scope of support. Our Los Angeles managed IT pricing guide breaks down what each tier should include.

How fast do you respond to IT support requests in Los Angeles?

Our help desk is in-house and US-based and available 24/7 to managed-service clients under contract. Calls are answered live during business hours; after hours, clients press 1 to reach the on-call help desk. Client support tickets have an average first-response time of 15 minutes; that is a measured average, not a guaranteed service level. For hardware or infrastructure work, our technicians come on-site across Greater Los Angeles from our Woodland Hills headquarters, with same-day on-site support when needed.

How do we get started?

Start with a free consultation. We learn about your organization, explain how we work and give you a clear proposal, with no obligation. Many clients have stayed with Pro Link Systems for more than a decade, including law firms, schools, manufacturers and healthcare organizations we have supported since the early 2000s.

Which parts of Los Angeles do you serve?

All of Greater Los Angeles: the San Fernando Valley, the Westside, the South Bay, the San Gabriel Valley, Downtown, Hollywood, and into Ventura and Orange counties. We maintain dedicated service pages for 39 communities, and our headquarters is at 21241 Ventura Blvd in Woodland Hills.

What is included in a managed IT plan?

An in-house, US-based help desk (on call around the clock for managed-service clients under contract), proactive monitoring and patching, endpoint and email security, backup and disaster recovery, Microsoft 365 and cloud management, network and Wi-Fi management, vendor management, and virtual CIO guidance, all under one monthly fee with no per-ticket billing.

Can you work alongside our in-house IT staff?

Yes. Our co-managed IT option adds our in-house, US-based help desk (with after-hours on-call coverage for clients under contract), monitoring, security and project capacity to your existing team. Your IT lead keeps control of priorities; we cover the gaps, the after-hours calls and the overflow.

The full picture

What are managed IT services, and are they right for your Los Angeles business?

With managed IT services, an organization pays a predictable monthly fee to a managed service provider that takes ownership of its technology environment. Instead of calling a break-fix IT company after something fails, and paying emergency rates each time, you have a dedicated IT company in Los Angeles that monitors, secures and maintains your infrastructure, with an on-call help desk for managed-service clients after hours.

The right managed service provider does more than fix problems. It prevents many of them, keeps technology aligned with business goals, and gives your team back the hours it was spending on IT issues.

Pro Link Systems has provided managed IT services in Los Angeles since 1999. We serve organizations across Woodland Hills, Burbank, Santa Monica, Encino, Sherman Oaks, Glendale, Thousand Oaks and Calabasas, with engineers who come on-site when the work requires it.

Managed IT keeps your technology running day to day. Deciding where that technology should go, including planning, budgeting, security strategy and the roadmap, is the work of a virtual CIO (vCIO). Growing organizations usually need both: operations from managed IT and direction from a vCIO. See vCIO vs. break-fix vs. managed IT for how they fit together.

Content Security

Secured Content Environments

Studio content has to stay inside a controlled environment from ingest to delivery. We build the IT controls that keep it protected across your network, workstations, and storage — and keep the environment documented.

  • Network segmentation & secured content zones
  • Access control, logging & monitoring
  • Locked-down, managed workstations
  • Encrypted storage & backups

Secure Handling

Secure Media Handling & Delivery

From ingest to final delivery, content has to move fast without ever being exposed. We build delivery and review workflows designed around studio security requirements that still hit your deadlines.

  • Accelerated transfer (Aspera, Signiant, MASV)
  • Encryption in transit & at rest
  • Forensic & visible screener watermarking
  • Expiring links & download audit trails
  • Role-based access to projects & assets

Our history

Built over more than 25 years, one long relationship at a time.

Pro Link Systems was founded in 1999 with a straightforward idea: organizations deserve a technology partner that stays accountable after the sale. At the time, most IT vendors were transactional. They sold hardware, collected a check and disappeared.

From a single office in Woodland Hills, Pro Link grew from break-fix support into proactive, strategic technology management, adding cloud, cybersecurity and, most recently, AI and automation as the problems our clients face changed.

Some of those clients have been with us for decades: The Waverly School for about 23 years, the Law Offices of Alan B. Snitzer for 18, and Cunico Corp. since 2012.

  1. 1999

    Founded in Woodland Hills

    Brian Shad launches Pro Link Systems to support businesses in the San Fernando Valley.

  2. Early 2000s

    Managed services

    The practice moves from break-fix to proactive managed IT with predictable monthly costs.

  3. 2010s

    Cloud and cybersecurity

    A full cloud practice is built around Microsoft 365 and Azure, with cybersecurity as a core discipline.

  4. 2020

    Remote work at scale

    Secure remote-work infrastructure is deployed for clients during the COVID-19 transition.

  5. Today

    AI and automation

    Private AI assistants, Copilot readiness and workflow automation join the practice, alongside the online Microsoft 365 Security Assessment.

The Pro Link Framework

Structured governance, from first assessment to long-term roadmap.

Every engagement runs on the same four phases. Executive technology leadership owns the framework; your leadership team reviews it with us every quarter, so problems are prevented rather than patched.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We document your infrastructure, identify risks and establish a clear baseline before recommending anything.

    OutputDocumented baseline and risk assessment

  2. 02

    Stabilize

    We fix what is broken, patch what is vulnerable and put monitoring in place across your environment.

    OutputA standardized, monitored environment

  3. 03

    Operate

    Our 24/7 in-house help desk and engineers run day-to-day operations against published service metrics.

    OutputMeasured, accountable operations

  4. 04

    Advance

    We right-size cloud spend, tighten security and maintain a one-to-three-year roadmap aligned with your goals.

    OutputRoadmap, budget and quarterly business review

Security & governance

How we hold ourselves accountable.

Clients give us access to their most critical systems. These are the commitments we make in return.

Measured response

Our average ticket first-response time is 15 minutes, 24 hours a day, and calls are answered live by our in-house, US-based team.

Verified recovery

We verify backups with real restore tests, not by trusting job reports.

Open incident response

Our incident response playbook is public, free and ungated. Read it before you ever hire us.

Your documentation

Your environment is documented and the documentation is yours: asset inventory, admin access and security settings.

Disclosed interests

If we recommend a vendor, we tell you whether we have a financial relationship with them.

Regular reporting

Quarterly business reviews cover what we have done, what we have caught and what is coming next.

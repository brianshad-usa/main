What to expect from a consultation

  1. A response within one business day. We confirm what you need and find a time that works.
  2. Your environment. We learn how your organization works and review your current systems.
  3. Your challenges and goals. We identify what is getting in the way and discuss your business objectives.
  4. Your options. We explore solutions that fit, with practical next steps, whether or not you engage us.

The consultation is free. The Microsoft 365 Security Assessment is a separate, fixed-price review that you can order online.

Organizations of about 10 to 100 users are our focus, and smaller or larger teams are welcome to reach out.

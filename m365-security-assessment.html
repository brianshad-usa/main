What we check

About 80 security controls across your Microsoft 365 environment.

  • Identity and multi-factor authentication (MFA)
  • Conditional Access
  • Privileged and admin access
  • User account hygiene
  • External and guest access
  • App consents
  • Email security: Exchange Online, anti-phishing, SPF, DKIM and DMARC
  • SharePoint and OneDrive sharing
  • Microsoft Teams
  • Microsoft Defender
  • Device management (Intune)
  • Data protection (Microsoft Purview)
  • Audit logging

Fully self-service. You never need to give us an account or a password.

    Our team approves every engagement before the assessment runs.

    Our team checks every finding before your report is released.

  5. Your report and review call

What's included

Included

  • Findings sorted by severity: critical, high, medium and low
  • For every finding, what we found and why it matters
  • An overall rating and security score
  • A complimentary review call: we walk through every finding with you, with no charge and no obligation

Not included

The report lists findings; it doesn't include instructions for fixing them. If you'd like help addressing the findings, remediation is scoped and quoted separately, at your request.

Your data

Read-only from start to finish, and you stay in control of access.

  • Read-only: the assessment reads configuration and security settings and changes nothing.
  • It cannot read the content of email or files.
  • Access is granted by your Global Administrator through app consent. We never ask for an account or a password.
  • Pro Link Systems reviews and approves every engagement before the assessment runs.
  • After the review call, you can remove our access in two clicks.

Questions

Who needs to do the setup?

Your Microsoft 365 Global Administrator. After you order, they receive an email with two steps: approve read-only access for our assessment app, then give it Microsoft's built-in, view-only Global Reader role. It takes about five minutes.

Do you change anything in our Microsoft 365 environment?

No. The assessment only reads configuration and security settings. It changes nothing and cannot read the content of email or files.

How long does the assessment take?

Once your administrator has approved access, the assessment runs and our engineers review every finding before the report is released. We email you the report as soon as it is ready.

What happens after we receive the report?

You book a complimentary review call. We walk through every finding with you and talk about your plans. There is no charge and no obligation. If you want help fixing anything, we scope and quote that work separately, only at your request.

What if Pro Link Systems declines my order?

Pro Link Systems reviews and approves every engagement before the assessment runs. If we decline an order, we refund the payment in full.

Can I talk to someone before ordering?

Yes. You are welcome to book a call with us before you order, though it is never required.

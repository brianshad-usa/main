Questions

Who needs to do the setup?

Your Microsoft 365 Global Administrator. After you order, they receive an email with two steps: approve read-only access for our assessment app, then give it Microsoft's built-in, view-only Global Reader role. It takes about five minutes.

Do you change anything in our Microsoft 365 environment?

No. The assessment only reads configuration and security settings. It changes nothing and cannot read the content of email or files.

How long does the assessment take?

Once your administrator has approved access, the assessment runs and our team reviews every finding before the report is released. We email you the report as soon as it is ready.

What happens after we receive the report?

You book a complimentary review call. We walk through every finding with you and talk about your plans. There is no charge and no obligation.

What if Pro Link declines my order?

Pro Link reviews and approves every engagement before the assessment runs. If we decline an order, we refund the payment in full.

Can I talk to someone before ordering?

Yes. You are welcome to book a call with us before you order, though it is never required.

Client Support

For signed Pro Link Systems clients. Your help desk number, support email and client portal access are in your onboarding materials, and your dedicated account manager can resend them at any time.

How support works

Urgent issues

Call the help desk. During business hours, Monday through Friday, 9 AM–5 PM Pacific, calls are answered live by our in-house, US-based team; after hours, managed-service clients press 1 to reach the on-call help desk.

Everything else

Email the help desk or open a ticket in the client portal, with your name, company and a short description of the issue.

Your account manager

Every client has a dedicated account manager who can resend your support contacts and portal access, and coordinate anything larger than a single request.

Security incident

Suspect a breach, ransomware or a compromised account?

Call the help desk right away instead of emailing, and tell us it is a security incident. Our public incident response playbook explains what happens next.

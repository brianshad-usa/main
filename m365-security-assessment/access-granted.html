Microsoft 365 access setup

Step 1 of 2 done: read-only access approved.

Step 2 of 2 · Assign the Global Reader role

Global Reader is Microsoft's built-in view-only role. It lets the assessment read your Exchange Online, Teams and Purview settings.

  1. Open entra.microsoft.com and go to Roles & admins.
  2. Search for Global Reader and open it.
  3. Click Add assignments, choose Select member(s) and search for Pro Link Systems Security Assessment.
  4. Select it and click Next. Choose Active and Permanently assigned, then click Assign. If you're asked for a justification, enter “Pro Link Systems read-only security assessment”.

That's all. We detect the access automatically and email you when the assessment is underway.

The approval didn't go through. It was cancelled, or the account used isn't a Global Administrator of your Microsoft 365 tenant.

Please try again from the link in your setup email, signed in as a Global Administrator. If it still doesn't work, call 1-800-890-6133 or email info@prolinksystems.com and we'll help.

This page is part of the setup for a Microsoft 365 Security Assessment. To start, use the “Approve read-only access” link in your setup email.

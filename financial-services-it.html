What Keeps You Up at Night

The IT Risks Financial
Firms Can't Ignore.

Financial firms hold exactly what attackers want — and answer to regulators as well as clients. Your IT has to hold up under both kinds of scrutiny.

Business Email Compromise & Wire Fraud

Attackers compromise email accounts, impersonate executives and clients, and redirect wire transfers. One convincing email can send client money to the wrong account.

No Clear Record of How Client Data Is Protected

Examiners, auditors, and cyber insurers want clear answers: who has access, what's encrypted, and whether backups actually restore. Guesswork is not an answer.

Ransomware Targeting Financial Data

Client financial records, tax documents, and investment data are high-value ransomware targets. A single successful attack can trigger client notification obligations, regulatory action, and reputational damage that takes years to recover from.
